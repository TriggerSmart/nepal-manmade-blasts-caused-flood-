What triggered the China-Nepal border mudslide?

By Meng Zhe, Xu-Pan Yiru, and Gao Jie | China Daily New Media | Updated: 2026-08-28 23:36
Share
Share - WeChat
CLOSE

As rescue teams from China and Nepal race against time, some online commentators have already tried to turn this tragedy into a geopolitical narrative.

The Chinese embassy in Nepal has debunked a social media rumor claiming that the deadly mudslides along the China-Nepal border were caused by the collapse of a Chinese hydropower dam.

·A Chinese researcher described the event as a cascading hazard triggered by an ice avalanche. The disaster originated in Nepal, but it went on to have a major impact on China.

·A technical team from Nepal's National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority traced the disaster to the area around Langtang Lirung, on the Nepali side of the border.

·The US Geological Survey reached the same conclusion after analyzing seismic waves and satellite imagery — what was first reported as an earthquake was actually a massive glacier collapse and debris flow.

China has deployed firefighters, PLA personnel and specialized rescue teams with drones and helicopters to the affected area. Beijing says it's maintaining close coordination with Nepal and stands ready to provide further emergency assistance.
