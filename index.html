Section 2 — Not just ice. The rock failed.

Cross-section

Langtang Lirung peak > 7,200 m. Failure about 5,200 m on the north face. Drop on the order of 1,200 m into the Lhende Khola. Kristen Cook: “The rock that the glacier was sitting on collapsed.”

Sentinel-2 Aug 24: snout intact. Landsat-9 Aug 26: the scar. Nepali Times: Chinese drone apparently recorded the rupture live.

USGS path: M4.4 earthquake product, then reclass to M5.2 collapse/debris flow. ICIMOD hydrology: Trishuli +9 m in 30 minutes at Galchhi, +7 m at Malekhu.

Section 3 — Same river, July 2025, different trigger

Second event

July 8, 2025: Pyurepu Glacier supraglacial lake on the Tibetan side drains in a day. Classic GLOF. Friendship Bridge gone. Crossing closed ~6 months. Reopened January 1, 2026.

August 26, 2026: ice-rock + bedrock off Langtang Lirung on the Nepal side. Same Lhende Khola. ICIMOD’s Saswata Sanyal: second catastrophe in 14 months.

Section 4 — Tunnels that are real, blasting that is rescue

Altitude test

Two true nouns welded to the wrong verb. Hydropower diversion tunnels sit at river level: Rasuwagadhi 111 MW, Upper Trishuli-1 216 MW, Trishuli 3A/3B, Chilime. Peak missing-worker count around 933. Controlled explosions in the paper are Nepal Army rescue blasts after August 26, not a construction shot at 5,200 m.

As of Sept 3, officials still hoped 46 people were alive in a Rasuwagadhi tunnel chamber with food and water. Thermal drones did not settle it.

The only flat ground in the gorge held a border crossing, a customs yard, a truck park, and hydropower works.

Section 5 — Manmade claims, run as leads

Four claims

A. Chinese dam burst. Embassy in Kathmandu called it fake news. Satellite geometry is a missing mountainside on Langtang Lirung, not an empty reservoir.

B. Medog / Yarlung Tsangpo mega-project. Wrong river, wrong basin, on the order of 600 miles away. Inference dressed as a receipt.

C. Highway / railway blasting weakened this face. Opinion essays exist. Takshashila: no major construction at this specific northern slope. Say it: inference, not established fact.

D. Secret tunnels under the glacier. Published projects are river hydropower, a proposed rail alignment, and the Belt and Road port. No primary blast schedule at the failure elevation turned up for this script.

Separate fact: China Digital Times documented topic-muting and rumor punishments after Gyirong. Censorship is not a borehole log.

Section 6 — The warning that could not arrive in seven minutes

Two clocks

Physics clock: six to seven minutes to Gyirong, which is the first settlement. Institutional clock: May 2026 Kathmandu meeting. Nepal asked for glacier and water data. China says it shared “in a timely manner.” Two Nepali officials later told Reuters they did not get much.

Lake sensors would have helped in July 2025. They were the wrong instrument for an ice-rock avalanche. Secondary debris lakes then paused rescue.

Payoff. “Why didn’t they warn us” and “the mountain gave no warning” can both be true. Missing instrument, right slope.

Section 7 — Who was standing in the channel

Categories

Pilgrims (Kailash season; India later ~275 missing in Nepal; China 261 foreigners from 23 countries in Tibet). Locals (Janai Purnima / Gosaikunda). Workers (279 pulled from hydro sites; South Korea flagged eight plant nationals on day one). Downstream body counts: Chitwan 355, Nawalparasi East 218, Nawalparasi West 208, Nuwakot 177, Rasuwa 127.

19 bridges. ~40 km of highway. More than 10% of Nepal’s generation offline. Gyirong 27 buildings in 0.7 km². Friendship Bridge gone twice in 14 months.

Section 8 — Proven, open, still moving

Ledger

Proven in the public science/official stack: rock-and-ice failure on north Langtang Lirung; collapse generated the seismic signal; minutes to Gyirong then the Trishuli corridor; real hydropower tunnels and post-flood rescue blasts; July 2025 GLOF on the same river; May warning-architecture gap.

Open: exact climate/permafrost mix; whether a brief landslide dam added a pulse; whether any county-level construction vibration weakened this face; why China’s confirmed-dead number stays in the low twenties.

Outro

The blasting in the record is the rescue. The tunnels in the record sit in the river. The failure scar sits in the sky. If a leak ever shows a blast pattern on that specific face, it goes on the desk the same way the USGS retraction did — as a document, not a vibe.
